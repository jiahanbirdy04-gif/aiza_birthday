Content Studio

Add everything here — no code. When you're done, tap Generate and you'll get a folder to drop straight into your GitHub repo.

How this works
  1. Fill in your photos, audio, music, letter and flower notes below.
  2. Tap Generate my site files — it downloads a .zip.
  3. Unzip it. Open your repo on github.com, drag the unzipped assets folder in, and commit (it'll ask to replace the old one — confirm).
  4. Your site updates automatically in about a minute.
